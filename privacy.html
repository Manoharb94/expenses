Privacy Policy

Our Expenses · Last updated 4 November 2026
In short: the app keeps a record of your bank payments so you and your family can see where money goes. Your data is used only to run the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared outside your family.

Who we are

Our Expenses is a household expense tracker built and run by Manohar B (“we”). Contact: er.manoharb@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider), your display name and the name of your family group.
  • Payment records: for each bank payment: date, amount, money in or out, payee name, reference number, the category you choose, and any note you add. We also keep the text of the bank alert the payment was read from, so it can be checked if it was read wrongly.
  • Bank statements you upload: the payments in the file and the opening and closing balances. The file itself is read on your phone and is not stored.
  • Plans you enter: budgets, regular bills, savings goals and lending records.
  • Technical records: when your email script or SMS forwarding last sent a payment, and a short log of messages that were refused (kept for 7 days, for fixing problems).

Bank emails and SMS

  • Email tracking is optional. If you set it up, a small script runs inside your own Gmail account. It searches only for emails from the banks you choose and sends their text to the app. We never get access to the rest of your mailbox, and you can remove the script at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • SMS tracking is optional and only starts when you turn it on and allow SMS access.
    • Android app: with your permission, the app reads incoming SMS on your phone (and, if you ask it to, SMS from the last 30 days) to find bank payment alerts. Each message is checked on your phone; only bank payment alerts (debited, credited, spent) are sent to your account. OTPs, personal messages and offers never leave the phone. You can turn this off in the app or in Android Settings at any time.
    • iPhone: Apple does not let apps read SMS. If you choose, you set up forwarding yourself with Apple Shortcuts.
    The app keeps only messages recognised as bank payments; anything else that reaches it is dropped immediately and its text is not stored. SMS data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

How we use your data

  • To show your spending, income, balances, budgets, forecasts and reports.
  • To share payments with the members of your family group, who can all see each other’s payments in the app. You decide who joins your family with an invite code.
  • To send you account emails, such as password reset links.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with anyone outside your family group, except the service providers below that run the app.

Where your data is stored

  • Supabase (supabase.com) stores the database and handles logins. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s web page. It does not receive your payment data.
  • Google, only if you set up email tracking, runs the script in your own Google account.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. Refused-message logs are deleted after 7 days. When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away (see below). Backups held by our database provider expire on their normal schedule.

Deleting your account

In the app, open ☰ Menu → Delete my account. Or use the web page at delete.html. This permanently removes your login and every payment saved under your name. If you are the last person in your family group, the family’s budgets, bills, goals and categories are deleted too. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data in the app at any time; export it from List → Export.
  • Stop email or SMS tracking at any time by removing the script or the shortcut/forwarding.
  • Ask us any question about your data at the email above.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing household money and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
