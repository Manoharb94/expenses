Credit cards that count once, not twice.

  • Bill payments are not. Paying the card bill from your bank is filed as a bill payment, so nothing is counted twice.
  • See the card on its own. Home shows how much you spent on the card this month, and each payment.

Money you lent out, and what you still owe.

Mark a payment as lent and Gharwise keeps the balance with that person. When they pay you back, you see what is still pending, and it never counts as your spending.

  • Lend out and lend in. Both directions, with a running balance for each person.
  • Private to you. Lending entries are visible only to you, not to the rest of the family.
  • Loans and EMIs too. Track a loan with its payments from the Budget tab.

Photograph a bill and the numbers fill themselves in.

For cash, shops and anything the bank never texts about. Tap plus, choose Scan Bill, and take a picture. Gharwise reads the amount, the date and the category, and keeps the photo with the payment.

  • Reads on your phone. The text is read on the phone itself. The picture is not sent to any reading service, and it costs nothing.
  • You stay in charge. The amount, date and category are filled in for you to check. You type who you paid, then save.
  • Works in groups too. Add a bill to a trip or flat and split it right there.

Trips, flats and friends, split fairly.

Start a group for a Goa trip or a shared flat. Add what each person paid, choose how to split it, and Gharwise shows who owes whom, with the fewest payments to settle up.

  • Equal, percent or exact amounts. Split each payment the way it really happened.
  • Only the group sees it. Members see what was added to the group, never each other’s other spending.
  • Settle with real payments. A payment you make to a friend can be matched to what you owe.

A look inside.

Real screens from the app.

Your messages stay yours.

Money data is personal, so Gharwise is built to take as little as it can.

We never ask for your bank login or card number.

Gharwise only reads the alerts your bank sends you. It cannot move money or see your balance at the bank.

The filtering happens on your phone.

On Android, only messages that look like bank payment alerts are uploaded. OTPs, promotions and chats from people are never sent.

Your mobile number is used to sign you in.

We send the sign-in code by SMS through a service provider, and the number is used for nothing else. Bill photos are read on your phone and are never sent to a reading service.

No ads, and your data is not sold.

There is no advertising in Gharwise and no sharing of your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Delete everything, any time.

You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app, or from the delete page. Read the full privacy policy.

Questions people ask first.

Is Gharwise free?

Yes. You can sign up and use everything described on this page at no cost.

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. There is no bank login. Gharwise works from the payment alerts your bank already sends by SMS or email, and from anything you add yourself.

How does it work on iPhone?

iPhone does not let apps read your messages, so Gharwise uses the Shortcuts app. You install a shortcut once, and an automation runs it whenever a bank SMS arrives. The setup takes a few minutes and the app walks you through it.

Which banks and cards does it support?

It is built for the alert messages most Indian banks and card issuers send. If an alert from your bank is not read correctly, send it to us with the card digits and amounts hidden and we will add it.

Can my partner use it with me?

Yes. Share your family code or link with your partner. You share the budgets and fixed bills, and each of you sees your own spending in purple and theirs in orange.

How do I sign in?

With your Indian mobile number and a password. When you create an account we send a code by SMS to confirm the number. If you signed up earlier with an email, you can keep using it, and the app asks you to add and verify a mobile number.

My partner and I made separate accounts. Can we join them?

Yes. In Family Sync, one of you enters the other’s family code. The other person gets a request and taps Approve. The family whose code was used keeps its budgets, bills and goals, and the new person’s payments move in.

Does Scan Bill cost anything?

No. The text on the bill is read on your phone. The first time, the app downloads a small reader of about 11 MB. It then works from your phone and the picture never leaves it.

What happens to a card bill payment?

Purchases on the card count as spending when you make them. When you pay the card bill from your bank, it is filed as a bill payment and left out of spending, so nothing is counted twice.

Can I use it only on the web?

Yes. Open gharwise.net/app.html in any browser and sign in. Automatic SMS reading needs the Android app, or the Shortcuts setup on iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu in the app and choose delete account, or use the delete page. Your data is removed.
